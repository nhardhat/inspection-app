1. **Update.** Accept the update and check Settings shows the new commit.
2. **Write.** With the **Pen** on, write a few words in your normal handwriting, dotting i's and crossing t's close to earlier strokes: every touch of the Pencil writes; nothing gets selected and Select never turns on.
3. **Highlighter and shapes.** With the Highlighter, then the Rectangle tool on, tap right on an earlier stroke: nothing gets selected.
4. **Select tool.** Turn on **Select** and tap or loop round a stroke: it's picked as before.
5. **No tool.** Turn every tool off and tap a stroke with the Pencil: it's picked and Select comes on (as before); tap open space to let go.
