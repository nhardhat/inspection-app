# Manual test plan: sharp zoom in the PDF viewer

Device: iPad [model], iPadOS [version]
Build/commit:
Tester / date:

The PDF viewer opens from Site memo, Export, **Preview**, and from Settings, About (User guide, Features and limitations). Reply with step numbers and what you saw.

1. **Update.** Open the app, accept the update if offered, and check Settings shows the new commit.
2. **Open.** Settings, About, **User guide**: pages appear one by one, each labelled with its paper size.
3. **Pinch around your fingers.** Pinch out on a word: the page grows around your fingers (not from the top left), and stays put when you lift.
4. **Sharp.** Hold still for a moment: the text turns crisp (no soft edges), even at the maximum zoom (1000%).
5. **Scroll while zoomed.** Scroll to another part: it may look soft for a moment, then turns crisp.
6. **Buttons.** Tap **−** and **+**: the zoom changes about the middle of the screen, and it stops at 100% and 1000%.
7. **A drawing pack.** Export a pack with a big drawing page (A1/B1), tap **Preview**, and zoom into fine linework and small text on the drawing: it sharpens like the user guide.
8. **Back and Share.** **Back** returns to where you were; **Share…** still opens the Share sheet.
