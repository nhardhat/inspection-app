Open https://nhardhat.github.io/inspection-app/ on each phone (add it to the home screen for the full-screen app). A phone holds its own data: start with a new inspection, or import a backup. Reply with step numbers and what you saw, per phone.

1. **Update.** Settings, About shows the new commit.
2. **Bottom bar.** Dashboard, Inspections and Settings are along the bottom, clear of the home bar, with the green online dot. Nothing scrolls sideways on any screen below.
3. **Dashboard.** The empty Dashboard reads "Everything stays on this device." After step 9, the tiles are one per row and the Continue tile's steps read Pre-insp., Inspection, Site memo.
4. **New inspection.** New inspection, create a project: the dialog fits the screen.
5. **Pre-inspection.** The header shows back and the title, with three equal step tabs below (Pre-insp., Inspection, Site memo). The project card shows Change project under its heading. Fill in job details; the keyboard doesn't hide the field you're typing in.
6. **Drawing.** Inspection: add a drawing PDF. Pinch to zoom and drag to pan; the tools row scrolls sideways to reach every tool.
7. **Pin and photo.** Pin tool, tap to place a pin, type an instruction, take a photo. The item sheet fits the width; Done closes it.
8. **Select.** Draw a cloud, then Select and tap it: the bar above it (colour, weights, duplicate and delete icons) is fully on screen, including near the right edge of the page.
9. **Site memo.** Create memo: the job details read as lines with Edit job details below; each recipient is a block (company, attention, To/Copy) with × on the right. Add a recipient and drag ≡ to reorder.
10. **Export.** Export PDF, then Share… and Preview. Export Word and the defect register.
11. **Lists and Settings.** Inspections: search across the top, each recent inspection shows its project under it and its status on the right. Settings: every card fits; photos say **Save to device**.
12. **iPad unchanged.** On the iPad, the rail is still on the left and the steps still sit in the header's centre, with "Pre-inspection" in full; photo buttons read **Save to device**.
