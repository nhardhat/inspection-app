**Include in export**

1. **Update.** Accept the update and check Settings shows the new commit.
2. **Tick a page.** Pages view, a page with no pins, its ⌄ menu: **Include in export**. Its thumbnail shows "In export". A page with pins shows "In export (has pins or markup)", greyed out.
3. **Several.** **Select** two pages without pins, tap **Include in export**; select them again: the button reads **Leave out of export**.
4. **Export.** Site memo, Export card: "N pages (K without pins)". Export: the ticked pages are in the pack. No notes box on them (unless the inspection has general notes).

**Sketch pages**

5. **Add.** A page's menu, **Add sketch page after…**: choose a sheet (A4–A1), orientation and scale, **Add**. A new page appears right after it.
6. **The sheet.** Open it: the calc pad strip at the top (job name, job number, SK01, REV NO A, your name as designer, the scale, the date; no PARTNERS), the Northrop wordmark, and a light grid. Compare it with the Bluebeam calc pad.
7. **Sketch on it.** Pin it, draw and write on it: all as on a drawing, sharp when zoomed.
8. **Sizes.** Add an A1 landscape and an A4 portrait sketch: the strip stays the same real size; the next one is SK02, then SK03.
9. **Change.** Its menu, **Sketch sheet and scale…**: change the scale (and sheet): the strip shows the new scale; pins and markup stay put.
10. **Export.** Export the pack: each sketch page is in it at its own size, with its pins and markup, and the grid prints lightly.
11. **Not a drawing.** Pre-inspection's drawings list doesn't show the sketch pages.
12. **Delete.** Its menu, **Delete sketch page**: it asks first if it has pins or markup, then it's gone.
13. **Offline.** In airplane mode, add a sketch page: it works.
