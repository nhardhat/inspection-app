Use an inspection with a memo, a few instructions (one with photo confirmation and photos), and a signature. Reply with step numbers and what you saw.

1. **Update.** Accept the update and check Settings shows the new commit.
2. **Make it.** Site memo, Export card: **Export Word**. It shows the file name, e.g. "SY000001-SIM001-A-Level 3 slab.docx".
3. **Share.** **Share Word file…**: email it to yourself (or save it to Files / OneDrive).
4. **Open it in Word on a PC.** Compare it with the PDF memo and the sample: red sidebar with the wordmark, "SITE INSTRUCTION MEMO" and the office block; header, recipients and visit tables; body with the item in italics, the * lead-in and bullets; signature, name, title; disclaimer; footer with the roundel, strapline and page number, as high off the page edge as in the PDF memo; no photo appendix (photos stay in the PDF). Anything that looks wrong?
5. **Edit it.** Change some text and a recipient: it's ordinary Word text. The fonts look like the PDF's (Figtree), even if Figtree isn't installed on that PC.
6. **Register logo.** **Export defect register** again and open it: the red Northrop wordmark sits at the top right.
7. **Offline.** In airplane mode, make the Word file again: it works.
8. **Recipients table.** In Word, the cream heading row has grey lines above and below it and between the columns, as in the PDF; To and Copy are centred.
9. **Desktop.** On a Windows PC in Chrome or Edge, open the app, then Export PDF, Export Word and Export defect register: each Share button downloads the file.
