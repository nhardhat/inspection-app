3. **Share.** **Share Word file…**: email it to yourself (or save it to Files / OneDrive).
4. **Open it in Word on a PC.** Compare it with the PDF memo and the sample: red sidebar with the wordmark, "SITE INSTRUCTION MEMO" and the office block; header, recipients and visit tables; body with the item in italics, the * lead-in and bullets; signature, name, title; disclaimer; footer with the roundel, strapline and page number. Anything that looks wrong?
