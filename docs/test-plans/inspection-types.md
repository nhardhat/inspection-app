# Manual test plan: inspection types

Device: iPad [model], iPadOS [version]
Build/commit:
Tester / date:

Reply with step numbers and what you saw.

1. **Update.** Accept the update and check Settings shows the new commit.
2. **Settings.** Settings, Prefilled messages: a new **Inspection types** group with General concrete, General piling and General steel, each with a placeholder line. Replace General concrete's text with two or three real notes, one per line.
3. **Pick a type.** Open an inspection's Pre-inspection: **Inspection type** buttons under This inspection. Tap **General concrete**: it turns dark.
4. **Its notes.** Go to the drawings, open **Items**: each line is a general note (lettered first), and every page's notes box lists them.
5. **Two types.** Pick **General steel** too: its placeholder note is added after the concrete ones.
6. **Remove, unedited.** Tap **General steel** again: its note goes, no question.
7. **Remove, edited.** Edit one of the concrete notes in the Items panel, then tap **General concrete** again on Pre-inspection: it asks first ("1 of its general notes were edited…"). **Cancel** keeps everything; **Remove** takes all its notes.
8. **Your own type.** In Settings, **Add** an inspection type, name it and give it a line: it appears as a button on Pre-inspection.
9. **Memo and export.** General notes still stay out of the memo and appear in the exported drawing pages' notes boxes.
